<span class="anchor" id="about"></span>

I am an MRes student in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Yingzhen Li.

My research focuses on building efficient learning systems with structured and persistent memory for long and irregular sequences. I study how compact representations, state-space models, and probabilistic inference can enable models to continuously update and retrieve information over extended horizons.

My recent work explores HiPPO-based functional memory, online Bayesian spatiotemporal modeling, and efficient attention mechanisms, with the goal of developing scalable memory architectures for time-series foundation models, long-context models, and AI agents.

## Research interests

Efficient foundation models, structured memory architectures, state-space models, long-context learning, time-series foundation models, and agent memory.

<span class="anchor" id="research"></span>

# Research

My research investigates how AI systems can maintain and use information efficiently over long horizons. I am particularly interested in:

- structured memory mechanisms for sequence models;
- efficient state-space representations for long-context learning; and
- online Bayesian inference for adaptive spatiotemporal modeling.

<span class="anchor" id="future-directions"></span>

## Future directions

I am interested in extending structured memory mechanisms toward time-series foundation models, long-context language models, memory-augmented agents, and efficient AI systems.
